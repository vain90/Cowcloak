- https://simpleicons.org/
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

The expanded local service-logo sprite is generated from `simple-icons-pack` 16.28.0, a redistribution of the Simple Icons SVG and metadata set:

- https://pypi.org/project/simple-icons-pack/
- https://github.com/2bndy5/redist-icons

Simple Icons and `simple-icons-pack` publish their redistributed icon data under CC0 1.0. The Simple Icons legal disclaimer also makes clear that the individual brand names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners and can be subject to separate brand guidelines or usage restrictions.

Moolias uses these marks only to identify the corresponding service in the alias-management UI and does not imply affiliation, endorsement, or sponsorship by those brands.

Brands listed by Simple Icons as forbidden, brands not present in the selected Simple Icons set, or brands for which the usage position is still unclear are intentionally not bundled and continue to use Moolias' neutral monogram fallback.
